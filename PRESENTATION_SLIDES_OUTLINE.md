## 🎨 Design Tips

1. **Consistent Theme**: Use a medical/healthcare color scheme (blues, greens)
2. **Visual Hierarchy**: Important numbers in large, bold font
3. **Diagrams**: Use flowcharts and architecture diagrams
4. **Screenshots**: Include dashboard screenshots for demo slides
5. **Icons**: Use medical/tech icons for visual interest
6. **White Space**: Don't overcrowd slides
7. **Font Size**: Minimum 24pt for readability
